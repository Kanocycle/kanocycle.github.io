# Equations of Stellar Structure

Hydrostatic Equilibrium
$\frac{dP}{dr} = -\frac{GM_r \rho}{r^2}$

Mass Conservation	$\frac{dM_r}{dr} = 4\pi r^2 \rho$

Energy Generation	$\frac{dL_r}{dr} = 4\pi r^2 \rho \epsilon$

Energy Transport	$$\frac{dT}{dr} = \begin{cases} -\frac{3\kappa\rho L_r}{16\pi ac r^2 T^3} & \text{(Radiation)} \\ -\left(1-\frac{1}{\gamma}\right)\frac{T}{P}\frac{GM_r\rho}{r^2} & \text{(Convection)} \end{cases}$$

# Equation of State (EOS) & Opacity

Ideal Gas: $P = \frac{\rho k T}{\mu m_u}$

Radiation Pressure: $P_{\text{rad}} = \frac{1}{3}aT^4$

Degeneracy Pressure (Fermions):

Non-Relativistic (NR): $P_{\text{deg}} \propto \rho^{5/3}$ (supports White Dwarfs)

Extreme-Relativistic (ER): $P_{\text{deg}} \propto \rho^{4/3}$ (leads to mass limit)

Opacity Sources: Bound-Bound, Bound-Free (bf), Free-Free (ff), Electron Scattering (e 
−
 ), H⁻ ion.

Kramer's Opacity Law (bf & ff): $\kappa \propto \rho T^{-3.5}$

# Nuclear Fusion

Binding Energy Per Nucleon
Fusion releases energy up to Iron (Fe). Fission releases energy beyond Fe.

Gamow Peak: The product of the Maxwell-Boltzmann tail ($e^{-E/kT}$) and the quantum tunneling probability ($e^{-b/\sqrt{E}}$) determines the most effective energy for fusion, E_0.

$$E_0 = \left( \frac{b k T}{2} \right)^{2/3}$$

Main Reactions:

PP-Chain (Sun-like stars, $T < 1.5 \times 10^7$ K):
$4 {}^1\text{H} \rightarrow {}^4\text{He} + 2e^+ + 2\nu_e + 26.73 \text{ MeV}$
($\epsilon_{pp} \propto \rho T^4$)

CNO Cycle (Massive stars, $T > 1.5 \times 10^7$ K):
Net reaction is the same as PP-Chain. C, N, O act as catalysts.
($\epsilon_{\text{CNO}} \propto \rho T^{17}$)

Triple-Alpha Process (He burning, $T > 10^8$ K):
$3 {}^4\text{He} \rightarrow {}^{12}\text{C} + \gamma + 7.275 \text{ MeV}$
